Privacy Policy

MyFuture CBC Revision App

MyFuture CBC Revision App is a product of Ibuka Technologies , an online education platform based in Kenya.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

The app supports two types of users: parents/guardians and learners.

From Parents/Guardians:

  • Phone number
  • County of origin
  • Email (optional)
  • Password

Phone number and password are required for login and account recovery.

From Learners:

  • Name
  • County
  • Academic details (CBC level/class)
  • Performance data (quiz scores, activity)

How We Use Data

  • Provide personalized learning content
  • Track academic progress
  • Enable competitions and rankings
  • Communicate updates

Public Information

Some learner information (name, county, scores) may be visible to other users for rankings, contests, and collaboration features.

Children’s Privacy

Parents/Guardians control learner accounts. A learner’s data is only created through a parent/guardian account.

Third-Party Services

We use tools like Firebase Analytics to understand app usage. This may include:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent in app
  • Device interaction data

Data Minimization

We only collect data necessary to provide our services, in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, except as required for app functionality described above.

User Rights

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction of your data
  • Request deletion of your data

Data Retention

We retain user data for up to 7 years after inactivity, unless deletion is requested earlier.

Data Security

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted during transmission (SSL)
  • Secure Servers: Access is restricted and monitored
  • Access Control: Users control their own data

Business Changes

If ownership changes, users will be informed and data protection will remain a priority.
